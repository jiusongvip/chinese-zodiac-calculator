# Site Vision: Chinese Zodiac Calculator

## Target User
English-speaking users worldwide (primarily US) who want to discover their Chinese zodiac sign, understand zodiac compatibility, learn about the five elements, or check annual horoscopes. They range from casual curiosity ("what animal am I?") to serious relationship/compatibility researchers. They search for terms like "chinese zodiac calculator", "zodiac compatibility", and "calculate my chinese zodiac".

## Core Value Proposition
The most accurate, feature-rich Chinese zodiac calculator that delivers not just your animal sign but a complete personality profile, element analysis, and actionable compatibility insights.

## Why Worth Using
- **All-in-one**: Sign + element + yin/yang + lucky items in one result
- **Accurate**: Proper lunar calendar handling with exact date boundaries
- **Actionable**: Every result includes CTAs to compatibility, horoscope, and deeper content
- **Comprehensive**: 12 detailed animal pillar pages with personality, career, love, and famous people

## Content Boundaries
**In scope:** Zodiac sign calculator, compatibility calculator, five elements, BaZi (four pillars), yearly horoscope, animal personality profiles, lucky items/colors/numbers, zodiac year chart, feng shui lifestyle tips
**Out of scope:** Western astrology, tarot, palm reading, I Ching divination, paid psychic services

## Content Columns
1. **Calculator Tools** — "What's my Chinese zodiac?" | Main calculator, compatibility, elements, BaZi, year chart
2. **Zodiac Signs** — "Tell me about my animal" | 12 animal pillar pages
3. **Compatibility** — "Which signs are compatible?" | Compatibility matrix, best/worst matches
4. **Horoscope** — "What's my fortune this year?" | Annual forecasts by animal sign
5. **Elements** — "What's my element?" | Five element theory, element calculator

## First 5 Pages
1. Homepage (main calculator) — highest traffic, core conversion point
2. Zodiac sign pages (12 animals) — SEO pillar content
3. Compatibility calculator — high commercial intent
4. Elements calculator — keyword demand
5. Yearly horoscope — seasonal traffic driver

editorial with cultural elegance, warm red-gold accent palette, minimal
